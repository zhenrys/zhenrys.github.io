# About Me

Hi, I'm **Henry Zhang**. I'm a first-year master's student in Computer Science at the [School of Excellent Engineers][cee], [Nankai University][nankai], advised by [Prof. Qibin Hou](https://houqb.github.io/). I earned my B.S. in Data Science from the [School of Statistics and Data Science][stat] at [Nankai University][nankai].

My research interests are **multimodal large language models (MLLMs)** and **reinforcement learning**. These fields are moving quickly, and I'm still learning and exploring. I've found Stanford's [CS231n](https://cs231n.stanford.edu/) and [CS336](https://cs336.stanford.edu/) especially helpful.

Outside of research, I love watching films, especially science fiction. Two of my favorites are Denis Villeneuve's *Dune* and Phil Lord and Christopher Miller's *Project Hail Mary*.

I'm always happy to chat. Feel free to reach me at [zhanghenryNKU@163.com](mailto:zhanghenryNKU@163.com).
